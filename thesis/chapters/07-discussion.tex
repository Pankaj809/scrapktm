\chapter{Discussion}
\label{ch:discussion}

\section{Evaluation Hygiene as a First-Class Concern}
The most consequential finding of this work is not embedding model selection but \textbf{evaluation correctness}. A retrieval pipeline may appear to fail completely (0.000 across all metrics) when the true failure is identifier schema drift between annotation and indexing stages. Research pipelines should validate ground-truth membership in the indexed corpus before interpreting semantic metrics.

We recommend three safeguards:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Automated assertion that every ground-truth \texttt{chunk\_id} exists in the FAISS corpus.
  \item Version-locked chunking: annotation and indexing must share the same chunking run ID.
  \item Lexical or embedding-based fallback mapping when schemas diverge.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Hybrid Extraction Trade-offs}
Routing text-based PDFs to PyMuPDF and scanned PDFs to Surya OCR balances throughput and coverage. For the curated corpus, 90\% of documents (45/50) are text-based and extract in sub-second time. The remaining 10\% require OCR, which is the dominant latency factor.

OCR quality directly impacts downstream retrieval. Garbled Devanagari in \texttt{LMC\_MUN\_004} chunks suggests that OCR post-correction or font-aware preprocessing may be necessary for production RAG systems.

\section{Chunking Granularity}
Semantic section chunking aligns with legal document structure but may produce chunks that are too coarse (entire sections) or too fine (single clauses). The Kathmandu Economic Act (\texttt{KTM\_FIN\_002}) alone yields 93 chunks from 74 pages, indicating dense financial schedules benefit from section-aware splitting.

Queries requiring exact numeric table values (Q009--Q011) mapped with high confidence because section numbers in headings survived extraction. List-extraction queries (Q001, Q005) suffered from coarser chunks merging multiple lists.

\section{Multilingual Embeddings}
BGE-M3 is the recommended embedding model for this domain given Nepali--English code-switching. However, its 2.27\,GB footprint requires deliberate offline caching on resource-constrained hardware (e.g., MacBook Air). The Hugging Face mirror strategy (\texttt{HF\_ENDPOINT=https://hf-mirror.com}) addresses download throttling without changing application code.

\section{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Corpus size}: 50 documents and 11 queries constitute a pilot benchmark, not a production evaluation.
  \item \textbf{Single-vector retrieval}: No reranking, hybrid BM25, or cross-encoder stage is implemented.
  \item \textbf{No end-to-end generation}: Retrieval is evaluated in isolation; answer correctness is not measured.
  \item \textbf{OCR subset}: Only five scanned documents; OCR failure modes are underrepresented.
  \item \textbf{Font encoding}: Some extracted text shows encoding artifacts limiting both lexical and neural retrieval.
\end{itemize}

\section{Ethical Considerations}
Municipal legal documents are public records. The pipeline respects robots.txt conventions and implements rate limiting during scraping. A RAG system built on this corpus should cite source documents and disclaim that outputs do not constitute legal advice.
